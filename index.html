The five reasons, in one breath

1 · Delivery is broken. Since Oct 5 the live sets pay $266–$410 CPM for 600 impressions a week each. $2,977 in 29 days bought 5 carts.

2 · The cold sets fight the wrong fight. Adset 2 and 5 are the exact Masterclass stack capped at 64, with Instagram and desktop on, and a video that made 3,946 clicks and 0 real checkouts.

3 · The numbers were polluted. 24 of the 49 checkouts and 43 of the 86 carts Meta shows for this campaign are your QA phone (Sep 20–26). The remarketing set learned from it.

4 · Attribution was dark Sep 16–29. The CAPI change on the checkout cut credited sales for 42 days through $11,000 of spend. Carts and checkouts kept tracking; they show the campaign was weak with or without the bug.

5 · The warm money was never spent. Two buyer files uploaded, five lookalikes built, Masterclass checkout lists ready: no live Blueprint set uses any of them. Two of the three October sales came within four days of the Masterclass attendee offer.
